Project Overview

Brew Haven is a seasonal coffee shop branding concept centered on Pumpkin Spice Latte. Inspired by the rainy season, the brand aims to create a warm, comfortable, and welcoming coffee shop identity.

Brand Concept

The visual identity uses warm brown, burnt orange, and cream colors to communicate warmth and comfort. Coffee cups, steam, coffee beans, and typography are incorporated to reinforce the coffee shop's identity.

Project Objectives
  • Develop a consistent coffee shop brand identity.
  • Create a recognizable logo for Brew Haven.
  • Design promotional materials for the Pumpkin Spice Latte campaign.
  • Apply consistent typography and color combinations.
  • Communicate the brand's rainy-season theme through visual design.
Project Deliverables
  • Promotional Poster
  • Banner Advertisement
  • Website Banner
  • Original Logo Sketch
  • Final Logo Design
Tools and Design Techniques
Logo Design Branding Graphic Design Typography Color Theory Campaign Design
Learning Outcomes

This project provided experience in developing a cohesive brand identity and translating a creative concept into multiple promotional materials. It also strengthened skills in visual communication, graphic design, and brand consistency.

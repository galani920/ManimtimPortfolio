PROJECT OVERVIEW

About the Project

Brew Haven is a conceptual coffee shop brand designed around the theme of a cozy rainy-season coffee experience. The project focuses on developing a recognizable visual identity through logo design, promotional materials, and digital marketing graphics. The Pumpkin Spice Latte serves as the featured product, reinforcing the brand's warm, inviting, and seasonal personality.

DESIGN DETAILS

Creative Direction

The visual direction of Brew Haven focuses on creating a consistent coffee shop identity across different promotional and digital materials.

Color Palette

Warm brown, coffee orange, and cream tones were selected to communicate warmth, comfort, and the inviting atmosphere of a coffee shop.

Brand Identity

The coffee cup symbol and seasonal Pumpkin Spice Latte concept establish a recognizable identity that represents the Brew Haven brand.

Marketing Materials

Promotional banners, newsletter graphics, and social media materials extend the brand identity across different platforms.

PROJECT GOALS

Design Objectives

Establish Brand Recognition

Create a unique and memorable coffee shop identity through a consistent logo, typography, and color scheme.

Promote Seasonal Products

Highlight the Pumpkin Spice Latte as the featured product through engaging promotional graphics and visual storytelling.

Maintain Visual Consistency

Apply the same branding elements across promotional banners, newsletters, and social media content.

Develop Design Skills

Apply graphic design principles, layout composition, typography, and creative visual communication.
